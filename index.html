The bit everyone asks.

Short answers. Open what you care about.

Will this get my Google listing penalized?

Not the way we built it. Google bans paying people for reviews and bans screening out unhappy ones. So the discount buys the contact details, never the review — and the review ask is identical for every customer, happy or not. Plenty of tools in this category quietly do the banned version. That is the difference you are buying.

Does it need a battery or wifi?

No. The chip is passive — it draws the tiny amount of power it needs from the phone touching it. There is nothing to charge, pair or connect. It works during a power cut.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes, with no app. Modern iPhones read NFC in the background whenever the screen is on. Android too. The page just opens.

What if a customer's phone will not read it?

There is a QR printed on the base as a fallback. It goes to exactly the same place.

Can I change what it does later?

Any time. The tag points at a link we host, so switching the destination — Google today, a booking page next month — takes one click and no new hardware.

Who legally sends the texts?

Your business does, under your own registered campaign, with consent captured on screen and a working STOP. If we run your list for you we send on your behalf under that same registration. Unregistered texting gets filtered by the carriers and carries real penalties, so this part is not optional.

What do you do with the numbers?

Nothing you did not ask for. They are your customers, on your list. If you leave, you take them with you.
