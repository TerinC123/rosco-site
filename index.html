Questions.

Does my staff have to do anything?

No. The customer taps the tag and fills in their own details. Nobody behind the counter types, imports or logs anything.

Will this get my Google listing in trouble?

No. Google bans paying people for reviews and bans hiding the review ask from unhappy customers. The discount is for the phone number and email only, and every customer sees the same review request.

Battery? Wifi? An installer?

None of them. The tag has no battery and never needs charging. It works in a power cut.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes, with no app on the customer's side. They tap and a page opens. Android too.

What if I stop?

You keep the list. They are your customers.
