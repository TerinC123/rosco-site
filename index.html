12 · questions
Will this get my Google listing penalised?

No. Google prohibits offering anything in exchange for a review, and prohibits screening unhappy customers away from leaving one. The discount buys contact details only, and the review request is identical for every customer. Several tools in this category ship the banned pattern openly — that difference is the product.

Battery, wifi or power?

None. The chip is passive and takes what it needs from the phone tapping it. It works during an outage.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes, with no app. Modern iPhones read NFC in the background whenever the screen is on, and Android does too.

What if a phone won't read it?

A QR on the base points at the identical destination.

Can I change what it does later?

Any time, in one click. The tag points at a link we host, so the destination is editable forever without new hardware.

Who legally sends the texts?

Your business, under its own registered campaign, with consent captured on screen and a working STOP. On Managed we send on your behalf under that registration. Unregistered messaging is carrier-filtered and carries real penalties.

What happens to the numbers if I leave?

You take them. They are your customers on your list.
